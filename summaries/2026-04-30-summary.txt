Subject: Morning Edition: 10 AI workflow shifts worth the design team's attention

Today's Morning Edition is ready. The strongest thread across this issue is that the major platforms are no longer treating AI as a side feature: Adobe, Microsoft, Anthropic, Canva, OpenAI, and Google are all pushing toward assistants that can act inside real creative workflows, move across files and tools, and reduce the amount of setup work before design judgment begins.

- Adobe Firefly AI Assistant and Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint look most immediately relevant for current production workflows.
- Canva AI 2.0 and Claude Design suggest upstream teams may soon arrive with more structured, editable first drafts.
- ChatGPT Images 2.0, Google Vids, and Veo 3.1 Lite make concepting and motion experimentation cheaper, faster, and easier to operationalize.
