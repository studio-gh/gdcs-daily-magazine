Editor’s letter

After the prompt comes the system.

The most useful creative shift in this selection is not that AI makes more finished things. It is that the output is beginning to stay open: layers can be recovered, motion can become a library, a branded frame can expose only the variables that should change.

The same logic runs through the strongest campaign work. Stone Island sets a graphic grammar, then lets collectors speak. Silpo keeps the range coherent while local jokes multiply. Gymtrack supplies a transparent layer so the user’s photograph remains the hero.

This is the studio opportunity: define what must stay fixed, decide what may vary, and make the handoff visible. The work becomes scalable without becoming anonymous.

The week’s lead / Recoverable structure

Creative AI is moving into the production surface itself. Adobe puts tools inside Gemini and Claude while keeping a path to editable pages and layers. Canva reconstructs generated images as designs. Figma turns motion behavior into reusable infrastructure. The competitive edge is shifting from the first render to everything a team can still do with it.

Photoshop, Lightroom, Express, Firefly, and Acrobat become assistant-native tools; Claude adds a layer-based Express editor and access to more than 80 Adobe tools.

Studio test: Run one branded adaptation in Gemini and one PDF-to-Express handoff in Claude. Judge typography, layer fidelity, and round-trip control.

Steal this move

Build three production lanes.

HSBC produced 70 stills across 10 cities without pretending one shoot could do every job. The system divided the work by what each asset needed to carry.

01
Hero

High-control shoots for the campaign’s anchor images and strictest brand moments.

02
Local author

Photographers with cultural proximity create specificity a central crew cannot simulate.

03
Licensed

Existing imagery fills range and context without manufacturing every scene from scratch.

Studio exercise: Before the next global brief, tag every required asset Hero, Local Author, or Licensed. Then assign budget and review rules by lane.

Debate item

When the audience makes the line, who owns the value?

PENNY’s Comment Coupons uses AI to score entertaining comments, then turns that creativity into coupons and outdoor media. It treats the comment section as a paid creative layer, not engagement residue.

For the mechanic

Recognition, rewards, and media placement make participation visible and can turn audience language into genuine cultural production.

Against the shortcut

AI scoring risks flattening context, humor, authorship, and moderation into an opaque ranking system designed for brand utility.

Studio position: budget attribution, moderation, consent, and reward mechanics into the idea before asking people to co-create.

Moodboard fuel

Eight ways to keep authorship visible.

Use these references for the move they make, not only the look they produce. Each one protects a human choice inside a repeatable system.

Gymtrack / The user’s photo remains the poster

Study: Transparent workout data sits over a personal image. Design share formats as layers with room for user authorship.

Figma Riffs / Process gets a publishing surface

Study: Ideas, animations, prototypes, and experiments appear without pretending to be finished resources. Publish one studio experiment weekly.

Ones to Watch / A living commissioning map

Study: Seventy-two creatives across 16 countries reveal both emerging talent and the visual tendencies entering the field. Build a six-person shortlist by discipline.

Anti-greenwashing / Art direction needs evidence

Study: Muted palettes, leaves, organic texture, and circular icons can imply claims a product cannot support. Attach substantiation to every environmental cue.

Jenna Arts / Imperfection begins with behavior

Study: Awkward poses, queueing, stumbling, and small social errors create character before texture does. Brief a micro-action, not an abstract theme.

Impressions of Japan / Let the grid carry observation

Study: Wide margins, two columns, Canela display, and Cabinet Grotesk body text hold city photographs and short notes without turning culture into styling.

Casa de Rose / Let the vessel finish the image

Study: Bottle curves and rosé color complete the label illustration. Review packaging on the exact filled object, not only the dieline.

Motion Sickness / Archive by behavior, not year

Study: Windmills, propellers, inflatables, and moving objects turn a decade of work into a taxonomy of play, mechanics, and analogue problem-solving.
