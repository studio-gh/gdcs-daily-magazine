Editor’s letter

The gate is the product.

This week produced no shortage of material. The daily system filed 65 story records across September 30, October 1, and October 2 alone. What it did not produce was a weekly decision.

A briefing feed answers what deserves attention today. A magazine answers a harder question: what deserves to shape the studio’s shared memory. The second answer needs a visible act of judgment, not an automated promotion rule disguised as taste.

This compact holding edition keeps the magazine architecture intact and turns the gap into a design-operations lesson: selection is not an administrative afterthought. It is the product.

Debate item

Should the magazine auto-promote the highest-scoring stories?

Yes / Keep cadence alive

Automation prevents a rich archive from going unused and may surface strong material that a busy team overlooked.

No / Protect the meaning of choice

A score measures editorial potential, not approval. Auto-promotion turns authored judgment into a polished recap.
