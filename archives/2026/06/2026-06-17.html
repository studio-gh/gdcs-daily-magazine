AI Latest

The ten core stories, curated for design relevance and anti-repeat distance from the last fourteen days.

Canva · AI assistants

Update: Magic Layers now follows AI images into Gemini and ChatGPT.

Canva says Magic Layers is now available to all users inside Gemini and ChatGPT, turning generated flat images into editable Canva designs with selectable text, objects, and backgrounds. This is a material update to the earlier Magic Layers story because the control layer is no longer only a Canva-side repair step; it is appearing inside the assistant surfaces where many prompts begin.

For the studio, the practical shift is simple: AI image generation becomes less disposable when the output can be decomposed, resized, localized, and brought back into a brand system. The key habit is still human: treat the generated image as a draft asset, then decide which layers deserve to survive.

Canva · Perplexity Computer

Perplexity turns research into a Canva-ready creative handoff.

Canva is now Perplexity Computer’s design partner, letting paid Perplexity users move from research, performance data, or a brief into editable presentations, social assets, infographics, and brand kits. The story is less “another connector” and more a sign that the research-to-asset path is being collapsed into one work surface.

That matters for campaign teams because the riskiest moment is often translation: insights become slides, slides become layouts, and layouts become channel assets. When an AI research tool can call a design system directly, Creative Studio’s value moves toward the quality of the brief, the template system, and final judgment.

Adobe Illustrator · 30.5

Illustrator’s latest release makes rough input feel more production-shaped.

Adobe’s Illustrator 30.5 release notes add Concept to Vector, which converts sketches and raster images into stylized editable vector artwork, plus Rewrite for generating, translating, proofreading, and resizing text directly in Illustrator. The update also surfaces generative credit availability from the Application Bar.

The creative impact is in the combination: mood-board fragments, hand sketches, and first-pass copy can become editable design material without leaving the vector workspace. For brand teams, this is useful only when the output is treated as raw craft stock, then pulled through typography, accessibility, and system checks before it becomes a deliverable.

Adobe InDesign · 21.4

InDesign brings AI text and accessibility prompts into publication layout.

Adobe’s InDesign May 2026 update adds Rewrite with tones, Fit Text, and prompt-aware language matching; a visual indicator that flags when cropped or refocused images may need updated alt text; better Save as New behavior for Express-edited images; and more intuitive Flex Layout editing.

This is a quiet but important print-and-publication update. It connects AI assistance to the places where editorial design actually breaks: copy length, image meaning after crop, linked image edits, and responsive export behavior. The studio takeaway is to build review checkpoints around these prompts, not to let them replace editorial judgment.

Figma · Release notes

Figma’s 300 MB video uploads make motion less like an attachment.

Figma increased video upload limits from 100 MB to 300 MB across Figma Design, FigJam, Slides, Sites, and Buzz for Professional, Organization, and Enterprise plans. It is not an AI feature, but it changes how AI-generated clips, campaign motion tests, product demos, and presentation loops can travel inside design files.

The practical benefit is fewer compressed stand-ins. More of the actual motion asset can live in the same collaboration surface as the layout, copy, and comments, which helps reviewers judge rhythm and composition instead of approving a placeholder and discovering the real edit later.

Microsoft 365 Copilot · Create

Copilot’s Scatter effect brings fast visual styling into the Create module.

Microsoft’s Copilot release notes describe a new Scatter image effect in Copilot Chat and the Create module. The effect applies a dispersed, stylized look to AI-generated or uploaded images, with Microsoft positioning it as a way to produce more distinctive visuals without leaving Copilot’s editor.

For design teams, the watch point is not the effect itself. It is the steady growth of quick visual treatments inside general work tools. The more these effects become available to non-designers, the more brand teams need clear guidance on what is acceptable for campaign art, internal comms, and presentation polish.

Microsoft 365 Copilot · Agents

Copilot agents can be given image-generation capability.

Microsoft’s developer guidance now documents a GraphicArt capability for declarative Copilot agents. It lets agents generate four images from a prompt, support follow-up modifications without losing context, expose content credentials, and share or copy generated visuals into other apps.

This is a governance story hiding inside a technical page. Once custom agents can create visuals, design quality depends on the agent’s instructions, brand guardrails, review steps, and content-credential handling. Creative Studio should think of agent prompts as part of the brand system, not just as productivity shortcuts.

Adobe Express · What’s new

Express AI Assistant is learning page operations, not just asset tweaks.

Adobe Express now lets users add, duplicate, and delete pages directly through AI Assistant prompts. The current Express update also adds click-order preview for presentations, expanded translation languages, recent projects on Enterprise Home, and easier access to company templates for Enterprise plans.

The story is a useful reminder that quick-turn design tools are moving from single-object edits toward document structure. For PMI-style campaign systems, page operations and enterprise templates are where the risk and reward sit: faster production, but only if templates, locked zones, and review expectations are already clear.

OpenAI · ChatGPT Business

ChatGPT Sites makes internal prototype publishing part of the agent workflow.

OpenAI’s ChatGPT Business release notes say ChatGPT Sites is in preview for Business workspaces with Codex access. Teams can ask Codex to create, iterate on, and deploy internal JavaScript or TypeScript websites and apps with hosted URLs, Sign in with ChatGPT access, and workspace-managed permissions.

For a design studio, this is most relevant as a prototyping and internal publishing surface. It is not a replacement for polished campaign production, but it could shorten the distance between a design-system idea, a lightweight internal tool, and a reviewable working surface for stakeholders.

Canva · Brand infrastructure

Canva argues AI brand systems need to serve people and machines.

Canva’s latest brand-infrastructure essay makes a sharp point for creative teams: brand systems now have two audiences. People interpret guidelines with judgment; AI tools read templates and rules literally, then guess where the system is vague.

That maps neatly onto PMI’s reality. The more AI-assisted production grows, the more a brand guide needs operational structure: template zones, locked elements, approved image behavior, clear copy patterns, and naming that tools can understand. Brand governance becomes something designed into the workflow, not something checked only at the end.
