Story 09 • Google • Mar 31
Veo 3.1 Lite

Cheaper video generation matters because it turns experimentation into something teams can actually budget.

Why it matters for PMI Creative Studio: Google’s Veo 3.1 Lite is not the flashiest announcement in the stack, but it may be one of the most usable. Google says it delivers text-to-video and image-to-video at less than half the cost of Veo 3.1 Fast, while still supporting 16:9 and 9:16 framing plus 720p and 1080p output. Adobe’s newest Firefly story is really about control, not generation.
